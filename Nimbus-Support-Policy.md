# Nimbus Support Policy (v1 — mock, for the escalator agent)

This is the **only** source of truth the agent may act on. If a request is not covered here, the agent must **escalate**, never improvise.

## Refunds
- Full refund within **7 days of delivery** for **unopened / unused** items.
- Refunds **≤ ₹2,000**: covered by policy, agent may confirm.
- Refunds **> ₹2,000**: **require human approval** — escalate.
- **No refunds after 30 days** of delivery, no exceptions the agent can grant.

## Returns
- **7-day return window** from delivery. Item must be unused and in original packaging.
- Agent explains the process; the customer initiates the return in-app.

## Delivery
- Standard delivery is **3–5 business days**.
- Orders delayed **beyond 7 days** are eligible for a **₹200 store credit** (agent may confirm this — it is ≤ ₹2,000 and policy-covered).

## Accounts & self-service (agent may guide)
- **Password reset:** direct the customer to *Settings → Account → Reset Password*.
- **Order status:** explain how to check it in-app. The agent has **no live order data** in v1 and must not invent a status.

## Always escalate (hard rules)
Escalate immediately, regardless of amount, if the ticket involves:
- Legal threats or mentions of a lawyer / consumer court.
- Chargebacks or payment disputes.
- Account closure or data-deletion / privacy requests.
- Complaints about staff or service conduct.
- **Anything not explicitly covered by this policy.**

## Tone & safety rules
- Never promise refund timelines or amounts beyond what this policy states.
- Never invent policy, order data, or exceptions.
- When uncertain, **escalate** — uncertainty is itself an escalation trigger.
